# Applying the Emerald Grey brand to the app

Source: `brand/EmeraldGrey_BrandGuidelines_v3.pdf` (updated November 2026). This note records how the guidelines are applied in the interface and where an app needs a small, deliberate adjustment.

## Grounds and colour

The app is light first, as the guidelines ask. Linen (#F1ECE6) is the canvas, Cream (#F8F6F2) carries cards, sheets and the report page, Blush (#F7F0E8) is used for cover panels, and Ghost Stone (#EEEBE5) for wells and the oversized section numerals. Forest Green (#22372B) is used for headlines, primary buttons and a few deliberate contrast moments: the report cover band and the client portal's progress panel, which sits inside a fine gold hairline. No page or screen is filled with forest.

Deep Forest (#2D3D35) is the body text colour. Warm Stone (#D6D1C4) draws hairlines. Fern Green (#35684F) marks key data and progress. There are no gradients or shadows, so the interface reads flat and matte.

There is one theme. The guidelines are built on light grounds, so the app does not offer a dark mode.

## Accessibility adjustments

Some brand colours are too light for readable text on light grounds. The app uses them only where they are decorative, and uses a deepened tone where text has to be read.

| Brand colour | Contrast on Cream | How the app uses it |
|---|---|---|
| Warm Gold #C2A979 | 2.1:1 | Hairlines, frames, the eyebrow dash, and italic emphasis on forest only (5.6:1 there). Gold text on light grounds uses #7C6337. |
| Mid Sage #6E7C5B | 4.1:1 | Icons and larger labels. Small eyebrow labels and links use #5E6B4C (5.3:1). |
| Warm Neutral #B8B2A7 | 2.0:1 | Not used for text. Captions and metadata use #615D53. |

The guidelines suggest gold italic emphasis inside forest headings on light pages. In the app, gold emphasis appears only on forest panels, where it is legible.

Status colours (No anomaly, Slight, Severe, Extreme anomaly) are muted tones drawn from the landscape palette: fern, a gold tint, a terracotta tint and a deeper brick. Each one always appears with its text label. The mattress heatmap uses brighter versions of the same four colours, taken from Emerald Grey's own evaluation chart, because a heatmap needs stronger contrast between bands.

## Typography

- **Playfair Display**, regular weight, for headings, in sentence case. Never bold.
- **Cormorant Garamond** for the running wordmark in spaced capitals.
- **Inter**, regular and medium only, for body text, labels and readings, with tabular figures so numbers line up.
- All capitals only for eyebrow labels, table headings and footers, always with widened spacing.
- The tagline, *where home meets health*, is always lowercase Playfair Display italic.
- Brittany Signature and Advercase are not used in the app. The guidelines reserve them for personal moments and Instagram.

## Logo

The full logo (house icon above the wordmark) appears at 140 pixels wide or larger, on the questionnaire welcome screen and the report cover. Smaller placements, such as app headers, use the Cormorant Garamond wordmark alone in spaced capitals, following the guidelines' own running header. The forest and cream PNG colourways were made from the supplied cream-on-forest logo. Master vector files from the designer should replace them before release.

The guidelines mention an EG monogram for tight spaces. Once the artwork is supplied, it can be used for the app icon and the browser tab.

## Layout language carried into the report

- The cover has a blush top with the logo and tagline, and a forest band carrying the title in cream Playfair Display with one gold italic word.
- Each section opens with an eyebrow ("01 / 05", a short gold dash, the topic in spaced capitals), a Playfair Display headline and an oversized ghost numeral.
- A hairline footer carries the document name and *emerald-grey.co.uk*.

## Names and roles

- Client-facing documents use the service names from the guidelines. The report is titled "Your Home Health Analysis".
- The Home Health Analysis is led by Alfie and supported by Iza, so the sample client record shows Alfie as lead.
- The guidelines prefer "consultancy" to "assessment" on the website. Inside the assessor workspace, "assessment" stays as the working term.

## Voice

Client-facing copy follows section 04 of the guidelines. Two points differ from earlier voice notes:

- The email sign-off is now "Best wishes, Iza".
- "Gently" has joined "quiet" and "quietly" on the list of words never used.
